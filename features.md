# HospitHub - Features & Sections Overview

HospitHub is a comprehensive, role-based hospital management application that provides tailored experiences for patients, doctors, and administrators. 

Below is an outline of the core features and sections available in the software:

## 🌐 Public & General Sections
- **Home**: A modern, vibrant landing page introducing the hospital's services.
- **About Us**: Information regarding the hospital's mission, vision, and team.
- **Departments**: A directory of various medical departments and specializations offered.
- **Contact**: A portal for users to reach out with general inquiries.
- **Authentication**: 
  - **Login**: Secure JWT-based access for all registered roles.
  - **Multi-Step Registration**: A comprehensive registration flow specifically designed to capture detailed personal and medical data efficiently.

## 👤 Patient Portal
The individualized experience for registered patients to manage their healthcare journey.
- **Patient Dashboard**: A personalized hub providing a snapshot of upcoming appointments, essential health statistics, and quick navigation links.
- **Appointments**: An interactive section to browse available doctors, book new appointments, and view or cancel existing schedules.
- **Medical Records**: A secure vault where patients can review their treatment history, test results, and past diagnoses.

## 🩺 Doctor Portal
A dedicated workspace for healthcare professionals to interact with patients and manage their clinical schedules.
- **Doctor Dashboard**: A centralized overview highlighting today's schedule, pending tasks, and patient load.
- **My Appointments**: Detailed schedule supervision for viewing, accepting, and preparing for patient consultations.
- **My Patients**: A directory of assigned or previously treated patients to quickly access their medical histories.
- **Prescriptions Management**: A digital interface allowing doctors to write, issue, and review medical prescriptions for their patients.

## ⚙️ Administrator Portal
The control center for hospital managers and IT administrators to oversee the whole system.
- **Admin Dashboard**: High-level system oversight summarizing hospital operations, staff status, and overall occupancy.
- **Manage Staff**: Comprehensive tools to onboard new staff, edit privileges, and oversee employee accounts (doctors, nurses, administrative staff).
- **System Reports**: Analytical dashboards to review hospital performance metrics and operational statistics.
- **Settings**: Global configurations for system parameters.

## 🚨 Emergency Ward Management
An integrated feature for handling critical care and facility resources dynamically.
- **Live Bed Tracking**: Real-time visualization of emergency unit occupancy.
- **Patient Emergency Requests**: Enables patients facing immediate crises to quickly request emergency beds.
- **Resource Management**: Empowers doctors and administrators to instantly review requests, allocate available beds, and update unit statuses on the fly.
